Same character, clothing, head and body proportions across all frames. Actual transparent alpha background, no checkerboard drawn, no background, no ground, no text, no numbers, no borders or grid. Eight temporally coherent DISTINCT animation poses. Never crop heads, feet or projectile.

Fixed front three-quarter camera and consistent identity, body scale, outfit, lighting.
